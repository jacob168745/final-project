## 📌 Project Overview (One-Sentence Summary)

A data-driven evaluation of Pittsburgh neighborhoods to determine which is the “best,” based on livability and community activity metrics using real city datasets.

---

## 📊 Metric Description — PLEM

We created the **Pittsburgh Livability & Engagement Metric (PLEM)**  
→ a combined score that determines how well a neighborhood supports an **active, stable, and connected community**.

PLEM consists of **3 sub-metrics**:

| Sub-Metric | What It Measures | Dataset Used |
|-----------|-----------------|--------------|
| **Activity** | Daily vibrancy, parking utilization | Aggregated Parking Transactions (WPRDC) |
| **Community Engagement** | Civic participation & social cohesion | Daily Community Center Attendance (WPRDC) |
| **Economic Stability** | Long-term business reliability & services | Licensed Businesses, Contractors & Trades (WPRDC) |

Final PLEM Score = Normalized (Activity + Engagement + Stability)
